Same app, opened from your phone — so you can screenshot a Naukri job and upload it straight away, no transferring to the laptop.

Then make it look like a real app

On Android (Chrome): open that address → tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → tap Add to Home screen → Add.

On iPhone (Safari): open that address → tap the Share button (square with an arrow, at the bottom) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add.

You'll get a Career Coach icon on your home screen. Tapping it opens the app full-screen with no browser bars — it looks and feels like a normal app.

Two things to know

1. Your phone and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi. This app lives on your laptop, so the phone is just viewing it across your home network.

2. Your laptop must be switched on with the app running. If the laptop is asleep or off, the phone icon won't load anything. That's the trade-off of keeping your resume data private on your own machine.
